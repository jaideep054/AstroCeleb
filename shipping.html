Shipping &
Delivery Policy.

How digital services and physical products are delivered. Last updated: 6 November 2026.

1. Digital services

Horoscopes and kundli tools are accessed in the app. Consultations are delivered through supported in-app chat or call features. Digital reports, where offered, are delivered through the method and timeframe shown before purchase. No physical shipment applies to these services.

2. Physical products

If you buy a physical item from the Astroceleb shop, the product page and checkout show where it can be shipped, the shipping charge, and an estimated delivery time before payment. Delivery estimates may vary by address, stock availability, and courier service.

3. Order updates

After an order is accepted, available tracking or dispatch updates are shared through the app or your registered contact details. Keep your delivery address accurate and complete. If a package cannot be delivered because the address is incorrect or incomplete, support can advise on the available options.

4. Delays, damage, or missing items

If your order is delayed beyond the displayed estimate, or arrives damaged or incomplete, contact support through our Contact page with your order ID. For return and refund eligibility, see our Cancellation & Refund Policy.
