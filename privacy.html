Privacy Policy.

How Astroceleb handles information when you use the app and website. Last updated: 6 November 2026.

1. Who this policy covers

This policy applies to users of Astroceleb’s website and app. You can contact us using the details on our Contact page.

2. Information we may collect

Depending on the service used, information may include your name, phone number, email address, birth date, birth time and place, profile information, consultation requests and messages, order details, payment status and identifiers, support correspondence, and basic technical information such as device and usage data.

3. Why we use information

We use information to provide app features, generate requested astrology content, connect you with astrologers, fulfil orders and reports, process or reconcile payments, answer support requests, prevent misuse, maintain security, and meet applicable obligations. Marketing messages should be sent only where permitted and with an available opt-out.

4. Payments and service providers

Payments may be handled by Razorpay or another payment provider. Payment providers process payment details under their own terms and privacy notices. We use payment identifiers and status for records and support. Do not share complete card details with us. We may share necessary data with astrologers, fulfilment partners, hosting providers, and support providers to deliver a requested service.

5. Storage and security

We use reasonable technical and organisational safeguards suited to the information handled. No online system can be guaranteed completely secure. Access is limited to people and providers who need the information to deliver the service.

6. Retention

Information is kept for as long as needed to provide the service, resolve disputes, maintain transaction records, or meet legal requirements. You can ask us about retention for your information using the Contact page.

7. Your choices

You may request access, correction, or deletion of your personal information, subject to applicable legal and recordkeeping requirements. You may also ask about consent or marketing preferences. Contact us using the Contact page.

8. Children

Children should use the service only with appropriate parent or guardian involvement and in line with applicable law.

9. Changes and questions

We may update this policy as our services or data practices change. We will update the date above. For privacy questions, contact us through the Contact page.
