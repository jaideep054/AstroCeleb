HELP & POLICIES

Cancellation &
Refund Policy.

A clear starting point for requests involving consultations, digital services, and physical orders. Last updated: 6 November 2026.

1. How to request help

Contact support through our Contact page with your booking or order ID, payment ID, registered contact details, and a description of the issue. Please do not send card details.

2. Consultations

If a paid consultation does not start because the astrologer is unavailable or a service failure prevents connection, you may request a refund for the undelivered amount. If a session ends early due to a verified service failure, the unused paid portion may be refunded. Completed consultation time is normally not refundable simply because a reading differs from expectations.

3. Digital reports and other digital content

If a paid report is not delivered within the time promised at checkout, contact support. If we cannot deliver it, a refund may be issued. Once a report has been delivered, it is normally not refundable unless it is materially defective or the wrong report was supplied.

4. Physical products

For products sold through Shopping, cancellation requests are accepted before dispatch where fulfilment allows. If an item arrives damaged, incorrect, or materially different from its listing, contact support within 7 days of delivery with order details and photos. Any return instructions and eligibility will be confirmed by support.

5. Review and processing time

Please raise a service or product issue within 7 days of the relevant session, promised delivery date, or product delivery. We aim to acknowledge requests within 2 business days and communicate a decision within 7 business days after receiving the necessary information. Approved refunds are initiated to the original payment method within 7 business days of approval; your bank or payment provider may take additional time to credit the amount.

6. Duplicate or incorrect charges

If you believe you were charged twice or charged an incorrect amount, contact support with both payment references. We will investigate and refund any confirmed excess charge to the original payment method.

7. Other rights

This policy does not limit any rights you may have under applicable consumer law. Where a product listing or checkout provides a more specific return or cancellation rule, please read that rule before purchase; it will apply subject to applicable law.
